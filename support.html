Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does veterinary verification take?

Veterinarians registered with the Department of Livestock Services (DLS) or Bangladesh Veterinary Council are verified within 24 to 48 hours after license validation.

Can I use DailyVet AI without internet?

DailyVet caches your recent consultations and animal profiles offline. For real-time satellite climate radar and live diagnosis, an active cellular data or WiFi connection is required.

Why did my prescription scan fail?

Ensure the prescription paper is placed on a flat, well-lit surface without glare. If handwriting is illegible, try uploading a high-resolution gallery photo.

Is DailyVet available across all divisions?

Yes. DailyVet covers all 8 divisions of Bangladesh (Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi, Khulna, Barisal, Sylhet, Rangpur, and Mymensingh) with localized climate telemetry.
