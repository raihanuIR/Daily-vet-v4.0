Persistent rain coupled with >80% relative humidity significantly increases fly and mosquito vector density, spiking risk for Anthrax (spore exhumation), Black Quarter, and FMD transmission. Ensure ring vaccinations are up-to-date and isolate symptomatic cattle immediately. Notify the Upazila Livestock Officer (ULO) of sudden mortalities.

Immediate Outbreak Checklist
Quarantine Perimeter: Isolate affected animals at least 15 meters downwind from the healthy herd.
Anthrax Caution: Never open carcasses suspected of Anthrax. Deep bury with unslaked quicklime (>6 ft).
Chemical Disinfection: Disinfect premises and water troughs with 2% Virkon or 4% sodium carbonate.
