Daily economic loss projections reflect average daily milk yield reductions (10–25%) during acute thermal and vector stress, veterinary intervention costs, and morbidity loss. Dairy cooperatives (Milk Vita, Aarong, PRAN) and livestock insurance underwriters can use these numbers for proactive supply chain mitigation.

NASA SEDAC Data Methodology
GPW v4 Gridded Population: Census inputs modeled at 1-km spatial resolution to estimate rural agrarian density.
DLS Cross-Reference: Validated against Bangladesh Department of Livestock Services national herd census numbers.
Loss Formula: Models lactating herd share (30%) × 4L/day × BDT 60/L milk floor pricing during stress episodes.
