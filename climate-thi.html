Veterinary Heat-Stress Interventions Clinical emergency mitigation guidelines

When THI exceeds 72 (Mild Stress), enhance barn cross-ventilation. When THI exceeds 78 (Moderate-Severe Stress), provide shade netting, sprinkle drinking water with sodium bicarbonate & potassium electrolytes, and adjust feeding to cooler dawn/dusk hours. Consult a registered vet for heat-apoplexy emergency.

Species THI Stress Thresholds
🐄 Dairy Cattle Normal <72 • Danger >78
🐃 Water Buffalo Normal <74 • Danger >82
🐔 Broiler / Layer Normal <70 • Danger >80
🐐 Goat & Sheep Normal <75 • Danger >84
Heat Stroke Emergency Action
Immediate Cooling: Hose down legs and flank with ambient water. Avoid ice shock.
Electrolyte Rehydration: Add oral rehydration salts (ORS) & potassium salts to ad-libitum water.
