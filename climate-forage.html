Bangladesh Agronomic Guidelines Pasture & fodder management practices

Napier grass requires well-drained loamy soil with 45–65% moisture. In waterlogged haor or coastal chars (Barisal/Sylhet), prioritize German or Para grass to prevent root rot. During winter dry spells (Rajshahi/Rangpur), mulching with straw reduces irrigation evaporation by up to 35%.

Rotational Paddock System
21-Day Rest Cycle: Rotate grazing paddocks every 21 days to prevent overgrazing and maintain root vitality.
Optimal Stubble Height: Cut grass at 10–15cm above soil to accelerate vegetative tillering.
Haor Drainage Check: Maintain shallow field ridges to prevent anaerobic stagnant root suffocation.
