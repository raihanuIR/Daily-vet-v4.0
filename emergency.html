Rapid first aid

Poisoning

Keep the animal calm. Do not force-feed. Note what it ate and get a vet immediately.

Bloat (cattle)

Keep it standing with the front legs raised. Do not let it lie down.

Severe bleeding

Press a clean cloth firmly on the wound and keep pressing.

Heatstroke

Move it to shade. Pour cool water on the feet, ears and neck.
