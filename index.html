Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using DailyVet on your phone or computer.

Is DailyVet free to use for farmers and pet owners?

Yes, DailyVet's core services — including AI clinical symptom triaging, emergency guides, community discussions, and NASA satellite weather alerts — are 100% free for all livestock farmers and pet parents in Bangladesh.

How do I get started with DailyVet?

Getting started takes less than a minute. Simply create your account, select the animals you care for (cattle, poultry, goats, pets, etc.), and you can immediately begin AI symptom screenings, view regional climate risks, or connect with licensed doctors.

How accurate is the DailyVet Clinical AI?

DailyVet AI is trained on certified veterinary literature and Bangladeshi field protocols, achieving high triage accuracy for common diseases like Mastitis, FMD, Anthrax, PPR, Newcastle, and Parvo. However, AI provides clinical guidance and first aid; we always recommend consulting a licensed veterinarian for prescription medication.

What should I do during an animal emergency?

Immediately dial the National Veterinary Hotline at 16358 or visit our Emergency section to view instant bleeding control, dystocia protocols, and mobile clinic contacts in your area.

How do doctors join the DailyVet network?

Licensed veterinary practitioners with degrees from recognized universities (BAU, CVASU, SAU, etc.) can register directly via our Doctor Portal using their phone number to receive clinical consult requests.
